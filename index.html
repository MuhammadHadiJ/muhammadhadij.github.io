02. Featured Project

Verland

In development — not yet live

A structured property-review platform for the Pakistani real estate market. Formerly called "Real Estate Reviews."

The problem

Platforms like zameen.com are ad-first, not customer-first. Reviews on generic listing sites read like "nice place, good area" — they tell a buyer or renter nothing about the things that actually matter day to day: does the water pressure hold up, is there load-shedding, does the elevator actually work.

The approach

Verland replaces the star rating with structured, category-specific signals: electricity reliability (load-shedding frequency is a real problem in Karachi), gas availability, water reliability, elevator presence, maintenance quality. Reviews are modeled as ternary/binary condition fields rather than a 1–5 scale, and aggregated to a single "dominant label" per field per property, plus spatially-grouped neighborhood signals that never duplicate the underlying review data.

The reasoning behind ditching star ratings specifically: a 1–5 scale is subjective in a way that doesn't compress well — what does a "3/5" on water reliability even mean to the next reader — and it asks people to pick from five options when the real-world answer is usually closer to two or three states (reliable / unreliable / occasional). Structured fields cut the ambiguity and the cognitive load at the same time.

How it's built

The backend is built directly on Python's http.server — no framework — with custom request routing, manual JSON body parsing, and ThreadingHTTPServer for concurrency. Supabase Auth's OAuth flow (Google as provider) is integrated via raw HTTP calls, including the authorization-code exchange and session/cookie handling by hand. LocationIQ handles address geocoding and autocomplete; Supabase (Postgres) handles persistence.

Scope discipline

The V1 spec has an explicit non-goals list — no star ratings, no feeds, no AI summaries — written specifically to keep the project shippable instead of open-ended. Given a documented habit of stalling around 40% on ambitious builds, that list is doing real work.
